# Faction greetings and farewells (docs/factions.md, "Voices"; Laz, 2026-10-12).
# slot | conditions | text | flags
#
# A person's people sets the rhythm (their own greetings, their own words);
# their faction sets the attitude. A line tagged with a faction outranks a
# plain one (`faction=` counts double), and a sub-faction's line names both
# the parent and the sub, so it outranks the parent's. Talk about other
# factions comes up only when it's asked about ("What do you make of
# things?", faction_talk.txt), not here.

# ---- The First Peoples: cold courtesy -----------------------------------------
greeting | faction=first_peoples, stranger | You may wait by the gate. Someone will see to you.
greeting | faction=first_peoples, known | Ah. You again. Well, you know the way to the gate by now.
greeting | faction=first_peoples, warm | You are welcome here, {w:friend}. That is not something we say lightly.
farewell | faction=first_peoples | Mind the road. It is not ours past the bridge.
farewell | faction=first_peoples, warm | Go well. The house will remember you kindly.
greeting | faction=first_peoples, faction=seated_houses, stranger | The houses keep good order here. I'm sure you'll do nothing to change that.
greeting | faction=first_peoples, faction=true_line, stranger | Our blood was here before theirs had a name. Remember whose hill you stand on.
greeting | faction=first_peoples, faction=tide_houses, stranger | The elders listen... sometimes. What brings you to the shore?

# ---- The Great House ------------------------------------------------------------
greeting | faction=great_house, stranger | By whose leave do you travel? No matter. Speak.
farewell | faction=great_house | Go by leave of the Throne, whatever you think of it.
greeting | faction=great_house, faction=throne, stranger | Kneel or don't. The Throne remembers both.
greeting | faction=great_house, faction=throne, known | The emperor's peace held this whole island once. It will again. What do you need?
greeting | faction=great_house, faction=banner_lords, stranger | My lord flies the Throne's banner. That counts for something here. State your business.
greeting | faction=great_house, faction=free_lords, stranger | What can you do for me, and what will it cost?
greeting | faction=great_house, faction=free_lords, known | Back again. Good. I like people who come back with something to sell.

# ---- The Open Hand: idealistic ----------------------------------------------------
greeting | faction=open_hand, stranger | Welcome. Whoever you are, your coin's as good as anyone's, and so's your word.
greeting | faction=open_hand, known | Good to see you on the road again. What news from the other towns?
farewell | faction=open_hand | Open roads, friend.
greeting | faction=open_hand, faction=open_council, stranger | Welcome, welcome. Every traveller who passes through is a thread in something bigger.
greeting | faction=open_hand, faction=sharp_hands, stranger | Ah, a discerning sort, I can tell. For you, a special price. Between friends.
greeting | faction=open_hand, faction=road_crews, stranger | Council talks about the road. We walk it. What do you need?

# ---- Minors ----------------------------------------------------------------------

greeting | faction=holy_house, known | The stone remembers the faithless. It remembers the faithful too. Which are you today?
farewell | faction=holy_house | Many have left the shrine. The shrine has not left them. Go.

greeting | faction=far_sails, known | Back from the dry land? Sit, there's always room on the deck.
farewell | faction=far_sails | Fair wind. Or any wind. We're not fussy.
greeting | faction=binders, stranger | You travel light. No one waiting for you, I'd guess.
farewell | faction=binders | Careful on the roads at night, friend. People go missing.
greeting | faction=wardens, stranger | Pay's bad, food's worse, and the trees want to eat you. Interested?
farewell | faction=wardens | Tell your lord the wall's still standing. For now.
greeting | faction=highfolk, stranger | Your road is long, lowlander. Sit by the fire and give us your name, and your father's.

greeting | faction=rooted | We are many. We are growing. You are... not yet.
farewell | faction=rooted | Lie down a while, when you are tired. The green is soft.
