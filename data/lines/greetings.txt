# Each people greets in its own way, with a word or two of its own tongue
# ({w:friend} is "friend" in the speaker's tongue; the player can hover it).
greeting | voice=roduro | {W:peace} to you. Mind your feet, the paving here is still growing.
greeting | voice=roduro, stranger | You're not from here, are you? Well, you're under our roof now, {w:guest}. What is it you want?
greeting | voice=roduro, known | Ah, you again. Well met, {w:friend}. Come, sit a moment if you like.
greeting | voice=roduro, warm | There you are! I was hoping you'd come by. {W:peace} to you, {w:friend}.
greeting | voice=roduro, morning | Early, aren't you? The stone's still cold. Go on, then, what is it?
greeting | voice=qotiro | Speak plainly. I have work waiting.
greeting | voice=qotiro, stranger | I don't know you. Say who you are and what you want.
greeting | voice=qotiro, known | You came back. Good. What do you need?
greeting | voice=qotiro, warm | {W:sun} on your road. It's good to see a face I can trust.
greeting | voice=qotiro, hot | If this is going to waste my time, say so now.
greeting | voice=horaro | Come in out of the wind, {w:friend}. What brings you to me?
greeting | voice=horaro, stranger | A new face! The {w:tide} brings all sorts. Welcome, welcome. What can I do for you?
greeting | voice=horaro, warm | Ho! Salt and fair weather to you, {w:friend}. Sit, sit.
greeting | voice=horaro, night | You're about late. The {w:moon}'s up, so it can't be good news. What is it?
greeting | voice=tadoro | Ah, a new face. Tell me something I don't know. No? Then I'll tell you something.
greeting | voice=tadoro, known | You again! Good, I had a question saved up for you. But you first.
greeting | voice=tadoro, warm | {W:friend}! Sit down, I've been writing all morning and my hand needs a rest.
greeting | voice=tadoro, stranger | Hello, traveller. I'd ask where you've come from, but I'll guess instead, if you don't mind. I'm usually right.
greeting | distrusts | I've nothing to say to your sort. Move along. | refuse
greeting | distrusts, voice=qotiro | No. Go. | refuse
greeting | distrusts, voice=horaro | The {w:tide} can take you back where you came from. I'm not talking to you. | refuse
