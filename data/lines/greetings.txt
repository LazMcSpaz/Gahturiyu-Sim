# ({w:friend} is "friend" in the speaker's tongue; the player can hover it).

# ---- More ways to say hello (RG-6) ---------------------------------------
# Nearly every line here has two conditions, so they all weigh the same and
# each person's fixed roll picks among the ones that fit them right now:
# their people, their job, their temper and mood, the hour, and whether
# they've met you. A line with three conditions would always win, so keep to
# two unless it should.
#
