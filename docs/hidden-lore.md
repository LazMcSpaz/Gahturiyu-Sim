# Hidden lore: the Womb, the One Race and the Overgrowth (Laz, 2026-10-12)
**Spoilers.** Almost nobody in the world knows any of this. It's the truth behind the Highfolk's secret and the Overgrowth, for quests, late-game discovery and the Rooted.

## The truth
1. **There was once one race.** The temple complex in the mountains, the **Womb of Creation**, is the supposed origin of the races. Its carvings or records tell of the creation of a **single** people.
2. **The gods split it into four.** Out of **hubris and envy**, the gods broke that one people into the four races: Roduro, Qotiro, Horaro and Ṭaḍoro.
3. **Unity is the natural state.** The four were meant to be one. This is not known.
4. **The Overgrowth is a wizard's spell, not the gods' work.** A powerful wizard learned the secret and tried to **force the reunification** of the races. His spell became the Overgrowth.
5. **He was killed by his own creation,** long ago. But his **animated corpse lives on through the Overgrowth**.

## Who believes what

| Who | What they believe |
|---|---|
| **Almost everyone** | None of this. The races have always been four; the Overgrowth is a blight, a curse, or the hostile plant-god Qotihìqì at work. |
| **The Rooted** | They serve Qotihìqì and the green, and hear the dead in it. They don't know a wizard is behind it. |
| **The Highfolk** | They guard the Womb and know of the one race. They believe the **gods** are behind the Overgrowth, reunifying all beings by force. |
| **The truth** | The Overgrowth is the wizard's spell, and his corpse lives on inside it. |

## Why the Highfolk keep the secret
- **They fear the end of their race and their ways of life.** If the races are meant to be one, the Roduro as a people would end.
- **They fear what the secret would do:** if people learned that one race is the natural state, many might **let the Overgrowth take them**, as a homecoming rather than a death.
- Among other reasons (open).

## How it ties to what's already designed
- **The languages already fit.** All four tongues come from the First Speech, "the tongue of the first men" (`languages.md`). Scholars treat this as a shared ancestor language; nobody guesses it was one people.
- **The Rooted's appeal fits.** "The dead return": the green keeps something of those it takes, and they speak through it. That's the wizard's spell at work: it absorbs people, and the reunion it promises is what the Rooted are drawn to.
- **The First Peoples' pride is built on a lie.** They call themselves the first two peoples. The Womb says there was only ever one.
- **The Writers' records:** a natural source for scattered clues (old texts, a scholar who got too close).
- **The Wardens** fight the Overgrowth without knowing what it is.
- **The Great House:** the empire's own court mage made the Overgrowth. Its fall and the world threat share a root.

## Decided (Laz, 2026-10-12)
- **Qotihìqì is real and innocent.** The god of hostile plants gets the blame for the wizard's work, from ordinary people and from the Rooted, who believe they serve the god.
- **The wizard was the old empire's court mage.** His spell may be part of why the empire fell, which ties the Great House's weak emperor and lost glory to the world threat.
- **The Overgrowth can be ended at its heart:** reach and destroy the wizard's corpse. A huge late-game goal.
- **The player can find the Womb,** but must earn it: deep trust with the Highfolk, or by force.

## Open questions
