# Guild Profiles and Story Kits — Draft (v5.1)

Content for the six joinable factions (the five guilds, and since v6b the Holy House, §6), in the format set by `quest-faction-stories.md`.
- **Sources:**
  - `factions.md`: voice, virtue, depth
  - `leaders.md`: leader archetypes, goals, foils
  - `faction-places.md`: headquarters and presences
- **Leaders are archetypes** (`leaders.md`). Each world fills each archetype with its canon person (the default) or a generated person who keeps the traits and secrets the stories below depend on. Either way the filler is pre-made.

**Reading the kits:**
- **Leaders are named by archetype** (the leader, the quartermaster, the old don, the showman, the sister). Each guild's header gives the archetype and its default filler; that is the only place a canon name stands for a leader. Pronouns follow the default; a generated filler's own are used on cards.
- **The throughline tags** (v6c; faction-stories §11a) sit on every entry, under the same names in every kit:
  - **enemies:** `shape`, `want`, `means`, `reach`, and **`reaches`**: the stakes its scheme can touch;
  - **stakes:** `hit`, `needs`;
  - **chapter-1 moves:** `needs`, `hits`, **`aims`** (the stakes it can be aimed at), `shapes`, `cost` (*yes*: open under *the cost*), `writ`, `limits`;
  - **crises:** `needs`, `hits`, **`decides`** (the stakes it can decide), `shapes`, `limits`, `writ`, `kills`. No crisis lists its enemies;
  - **endings:** `fits`; **odd jobs:** `thread`, **`fits`** (v6d: the enemies whose scheme the Hand's job serves), and sometimes `needs`;
  - **chapter-2 blows:** each kit's *Chapter-2 blows* table gives every event's `needs` in full, and *Per-enemy blows* lists, for an enemy whose means alone mislead, the only blows it takes. **How a blow fell** (held by men, by a bought debt or under a charge; wounded by men or through something rigged) sets its means and its door: assembly §4, *Doors by how the blow fell* (v6d);
  - **hearts** (v6d): `member` (bound by the guild's code) or `outside` (a client or a member's kin). Only a `member` heart is *disgraced* under the code;
  - **crises** (v6d): **`heart`**, the hearts the crisis can take (ids, a variant letter where only that variant fits, or *any*), and ***herself*** where the heart must be free to act, or is herself accused, sold or taken. A *herself* crisis never follows a *captive* heart.
- **Reading the tags:**
  - `needs`: `a` or `b` is either one; `a` + `b` is both. A bracket after a choice limits it: to a shape or named enemies (`men` (Above)), to region reach (`paper` (region)), or away from some (never Within, never W6). After a semicolon, a limit holds for the whole cell. A stake's `needs` is a list: any one is enough.
  - `shapes`: the enemy shapes the part rolls with; *any* is all three.
  - `aims` / `decides` *any*: every stake the enemy reaches. Only a part whose hits include `people` takes *any*.
  - `limits`: wants (`wants greed, ambition`), named enemies (`never OH1`, `only OH1`), *not under the cost*, *on S1, S4: …* for a limit on some stakes only, and the paired syndicates' *paired only* and *also paired*.
  - `writ` *yes*: the part counts toward writ fatigue when its `uses` is `paper` (or the house's `faith`, used as a writ or ban against the guild). A guild-target blow carried by `paper` always counts (faction-stories §9).
  - `fits`: hits, crisis families, crisis ids, *any*, or *paired*; *never D3* bars one enemy.
- **Readings:** under each move and crisis table, a reading per stake it aims at or decides, saying what the act is for that stake. Where it reads the same for every stake, the table's words are the reading.
- **Every signature premise has a Truth column** (rulebook, *No blank card*): the values it rolls, and the ways that reach them (rulebook, *The truth is reachable*). Each value is a truth row in the world ledger, tagged with its shape slug or marked *(confirming)* (rulebook, *Shapes*). A card writes it as `<signature premise>: <row>` (`The score: a copy`).
- **Never name the stake on a routine card.** Routine cards may mention the stake's kind (lamps, ladders, letters) in passing, but never the stake entry itself.
- **Variants:** under each pool, every entry has 2–3 variants (*a*, *b*, *c*). Roll one with the entry (faction-stories §11, *Variants*). A variant changes who, why or how, never the entry's shape, want, kind or compatibility. **Crisis variants end with the giver.** Chapter-1 moves have no variants, so a rolled move is recorded by its id alone (`M3`) (v6d).
  - **A giver needs a reason** (v6d): a giver who isn't the heart, a leader or an elder member is someone the last move touched, and a bracket ties them to the story: (on S2) rolls only on that stake, (hearts H1, H2) only with those hearts.
  - **Readings by enemy and variant** (v6d): where a variant changes *what* the scheme moves (debts or boundary readings, a guard post or a purse), or an enemy reads a part its own way, the kit's *Readings by enemy and variant* list gives the words, and the throughline is written from them.
- **Seat endings** (*take the road*, *take the gate*, *take the shop*, *take the ring*, *become its voice*) are offered only when the leader is the enemy, always as a **fourth** ending beside the 3 rolled, never among them (v6c; faction-stories §6, phase 4).

**Rules for every kit:**
- **Canon secrets stay late-game.**
  - No revelation publishes anything in `hidden-lore.md` (the one race, the Womb, the court mage and the corpse at the heart).
  - No faction-story card is pinned at Highcrossing's sealed tower.
  - The Wayhouse's empty-shelf hook is late-game, and no faction story publishes it.
- **Own places only.** A faction story's revelation may publish secret hooks on its own faction's places, never another faction's.
- **Depth used.** Only the Writers and the Holy House have a hidden side in `factions.md` (the Holy House's: its sincere priests). The other guilds' phase-3 private side comes from the depth notes listed in each profile, or from their code.
- **Odd jobs** put the Hand or a proxy in a **hidden** role: named in the truth, or holding a slot whose link to the enemy is a secret fact.
- **Event ceiling** (see faction-stories, *Event ceiling*): no kit entry produces a Throne edict, realm law or war writ. An *Above* enemy acts through a local instrument: a magistrate's writ, a lord's seizure, one march's muster. **A writ binds only inside its own march or lordship**, so whoever and whatever it names lies there.
- **One enemy side per faction:** a faction is the enemy's side in at most one guild story per world (faction-stories §8, *Enemy factions*). A *Within* enemy makes its own faction the enemy's side. Every enemy with a side, so the bars read the same both ways (v6c):

- **Chapter-2 blows** (faction-stories §6, *Phase 3*; doors in assembly §4, *Guild chapter-2 doors*): each kit's *Chapter-2 blows* table lists the target + event combinations it allows, what each needs, and **which leaders may be struck down**. A struck-down leader is never the story's enemy. A killed one carries `dies_at`, is met on an earlier card, never gives the crisis, and leaves a seat successor. A door's family is never one the profile marks *never*.
- **Under the cost, the blow protects what pays** (v6d): every *cost* enemy has a *Per-enemy blows* line, and never takes a blow that would shut the trade, hall or people its living comes from (faction-stories §11a, point 5).
- **The syndicates' codes bind their own** (v6d): an Old Hands or Night Market enemy *Within* never has anyone arrested, nor members taken by the watch (never go to the law; never inform).

## 1. The Writers
*Scholars' and mages' guild. Virtue: they keep the records. Hidden side: knowledge is power; they trade secrets and quietly steer events. Leader archetype: **the leader**, the Writers' head, always on the road (default: Fiyeweya of No Road).*

### Profile
| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Weights** | high: Investigate, Acquire · normal: Dispute, Passage, Restore · low: Rescue, Threat, Reckoning · never: Contest |
| **Methods** | Investigate: examine, question · Acquire: trade, deceive · Passage: cover · Dispute: truth |
| **Code** | **Never destroy a text; never take a side in public.** Removes ways that burn or destroy a record, and Dispute's *side* ways. (The *destroyed* ending stays, since it's a failure.) |
| **Vocabulary** | things: letters, ledgers, copies, maps, field journals, spell manuals · sites: reading rooms, letter-writers' stalls, survey camps, digs, relay lofts · clients: heirs who need a record, clerks, lords who want a history written their way, Wardens who want the green's edge mapped |
| **Motives** | weight up reasons about legacy, obligation and curiosity; culprit motives *cover-up* and *ambition* |
| **Depth used** | hidden side: knowledge is power |

**Twist rows:**
- **Copy it before it burns:** never *meant for destruction* or *wants to be rid of it* (the holder openly wants it gone). *Load-bearing* and *rightful keeper* are allowed.
- **The ledger doesn't add up:** never *no crime* (the record was altered). The other Investigate twists are allowed.
- **Samples from the green:** Passage's *Unwelcome cargo* column applies.

**Faction twists:**

| Twist | What it is | Requires | Families | Group |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Someone's name is in it** | the record the job turns up names a Low or Middle bystander in a way that ruins them if read out (a parent who wasn't, a debt they hid), and they have nothing to do with the job | a bystander slot and a secret fact on them | Investigate, Acquire | innocents pay |
| **Someone was told first** | the Writers already sold this answer to someone else, before the job was given | a person who profits from knowing | Investigate, Dispute | you started it |

## 5. The Dueling Society
*Combat as sport. Mocked by the old heads, and quietly deadly. Leader archetypes: **the showman**, the co-founder brother who runs the show (default: Liʻo of the Ring), and **the sister**, his adopted sister and co-founder, who once killed in a duel and has the trait *known fighter* (default: Kitim Duel-ender).
