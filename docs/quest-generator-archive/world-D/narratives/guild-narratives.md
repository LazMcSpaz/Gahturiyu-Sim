## Repetition check

**Per guild** (fixed · repeated · changed)
- **Writers:** routine, endings, a burning at the end · crisis C1 three times, C1a with the same giver and enemy twice; heart captive three times · enemy, blow.
- **Lantern:** routine, endings, the quartermaster's reveal · neutrality stake three times; C1b twice; odd job O2a twice · enemy, blow.
- **Old Hands:** routine, endings · paired three times, so OH2 three times (OH2a twice); lineage-roll odd job and river-house heir twice each · the paired crisis; one unpaired story (OH4).
- **Night Market:** routine, endings, the leader's reveal · N1 three times, N1c twice running; a toll lord's writ twice · the paired crisis; one N3 story.
- **Dueling:** endings · D5 three times (twice by elimination: Dueling rolled last), so the sister's blood bout three times · only 7104 (D2) differs.
- **Holy House:** routine, endings, the high priest's reveal · tithe odd job four times (chance); HH3a with C4 twice; the hill-road shrine keeper twice · enemy, blow.

**Across guilds**
- Never rolled, never varies: routine work, chapter 1's move, endings, the crisis shortcut. Every odd job hints alike: someone secretly works for the enemy.
- Writs everywhere: a guild outlawed in every world, always pleaded before a lord's court; men arriving at headquarters under a paper.
- A heart captive in every world; most last moves end "…and the heart is named / ruined / among them."
- Over 400 seeds the skews are built in: Lantern C1 70%, D5 39%, Holy House C4 34%. Blows are dealt evenly.

**Verdict.** Variety works between guilds and in the chapter-2 blow, but not within a guild: Writers, Lantern, Dueling and the paired syndicates repeat their crisis in two or three of four worlds. Nothing varies the routine work, chapter 1's move, the endings or the shortcut, so a guild's stories read alike before the crisis even repeats. Fix Dueling and Lantern first (wider pools, and a roll order that doesn't leave the last guild one enemy), then give chapter 1's move a table.
