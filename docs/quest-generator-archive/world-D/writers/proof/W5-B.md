### Branch a × E1 · Freed

*Blow a: guild · leader struck down · wounded by men on the road (`wounded-men`) → door Passage Fugitive. Ending shown: Freed.*

- **R1 · `{home_hall}`.** At `{home_hall}` a pupil's family means to burn a grandmother's prayer-book with Ṭaḍoro notes in its margins, and the heart's elder pupil carries it to the hall to be copied first (Acquire *Copy it before it burns*), and the heart, the reading master who recruited the player, wants it copied whole and says what she wants most, to read one roll whole, once in her life (her want), and that she was raised in the Mount's orphan school and left it for the halls (her past), while the family's word for the book is "'unclean', said of a book with a Writers' hand in it".
- **R2 · `{reader_lord}`.** From the seat of `{reader_lord}` the player carries home to `{home_hall}`, through the house's land, a crate of the dig's earlier finds that were lent to the lord's archive to be read aloud to him (Passage *Smuggling*), and the lord, whose archive is read to him, asks to hear the dig's next roll the day it comes out of the ground, while the crate's lodging list shows ∅ "a lodging list with one line left blank, 'to be read at the Wayhouse'".
- **R3 · `{lantern_hall}`.** At the dig's Lantern chapter house in `{lantern_hall}` the captain of the dig's contract camp means to burn the dig's day-book, and the player copies it before it burns (Acquire *Copy it before it burns*), "a dig day-book burned as unclean", while in secret the inquisitor's clerk (the Hand) told the captain the day-book was unclean and copied it first, because it is the one record that the roll came out of the dig, in which trench, under a Writers' reader, and the copy tells the inquisitor where the roll lies.
- **Reveal · `{home_hall}`.** At `{home_hall}` the heart asks the player to help her meet her orphan-school sister, now the Mount's novice-mistress, who has come down from the Mount and to whom she hasn't spoken since she left it (Restore *Estrangement*), and reconciled, the sister tells what she has seen herself, the inquisitor's clerk asking the Mount's archive for the old name of a drafting house and riders sent to the heart's dig, while the heart adds, from her own season as its reader, that the dig is the ruin of the house where the Mount's grants were drafted, whose roll she has begun to read, and the sister adds that the leader is riding to claim the roll for the Wayhouse.
  The house's word for a book with a Writers' hand, "'unclean', said of a book with a Writers' hand", is the word it will use for the dig, the burned day-book, "a dig day-book burned as unclean", is why nobody will be able to say a roll came out of it, and the blank line, "a lodging list with one line left blank, 'to be read at the Wayhouse'", is the line the roll was to fill.
- **Chapter 1's move.** The inquisitor's men close the dig as unclean, seize its finds and turn the diggers off.
- **C1-1 · `{dig}`.** Out at the closed dig the halls and the parish chapel beside it both claim the dig's ground under the old grant of `{dig_lord}` (Dispute *Shared resource*), and the player walks the old line with the oldest local before the lord's reeve, "the oldest local, who calls the ruin 'the scribes' house'".
- **C1-2 · `{dig}`.** But the inquisitor's clerk (the Hand) comes with the house's men to fill the trench, and the player must measure the footings against the old plan before they do, to show where the roll lay (Acquire *Take the readings*), finding "the hearth-niche of the scribes' house", {niche_look}, while the diggers say she copied the first page by lamplight before she read it to them: "her copy of the first page".
- **C1-3 · `{dig}`.** Therefore, as the clerk takes the diggers who heard her read for questioning, a digger who can say the first lines with her, who can't read and stayed to hear a roll read once, must be got away before he is taken, and won't leave the place the roll was read (Passage *Unwilling passenger*), and he asks the player to keep his name from the house, and for that he will stand at the Mount's gate when it counts.
- **C2-0 · `{dig}`.** At pressure two the player watches the parish chapel beside the dig, where the inquisitor's clerk questions the turned-off diggers one by one (Investigate *Watch them*), and sees "a list of the diggers who heard her read".
- **Blow and door (a) · `{dig}`.** On the road by the dig the leader, riding to claim the roll for the Wayhouse, is wounded by the inquisitor's men, and the door is getting her away from them (Passage *Fugitive*, the wounded leader as cargo), with her saddlebag that she won't let out of sight, and on it the heart's sister sends word from the Mount that the house means the heart to recant at `{feast}`: "the feast-day recanting".
- **Code-bending way.** The door carries one extra way that destroys a text: burn the clerk's list of the diggers who heard her read, so the house can't name them.
- **Crisis · at `{mount}`.** The roll has been burned in the Mount's yard, and the heart, taken from her lesson at `{home_hall}` on the inquisitor's charge of reading and copying unclean writing, is held at the Mount until she recants at `{feast}`, `{days}` days off, the charge resting on her copy of the first page because the roll is ash and the house holds a reader only on the page itself, so at the Mount's gate, {gate_look}, her elder pupil, who followed the riders there, gives the card because she was taken from his lesson (Rescue *Imprisoned unjustly*, the inquisitor holding the house's authority): get her out, or let her speak.
  - *How it reads blow a:* With the leader wounded and laid on a sickbed, the guild's word reaches the Mount's gate only by her letter, and the letter of the lord who asked for the roll rides in her saddlebag.
  - *What the player holds:* The roll was seized with the dig on the move, before the player reached it, and the house holds a reader on its own authority in its own land, so the footings the player measured show where a roll lay and not what it said, the chapel's list says who heard her and not what she read, her own copy of the first page is the very thing the charge rests on, and the sister's warning gives the feast day, which is the crisis's clock and not a way to stop the taking.
  - *The tell:* She sends back the rope her pupil passed in through her sister, with a request for paper, for she did read the roll aloud and means to again.
  - *The ways:* The ways are burning what the charge names, her copy of the first page, so there is nothing to hold her on (the code-break), the digger and the diggers on the chapel's list crowding the Mount's gate, which makes the hold cost the house (his price: his name kept, which the crowd can't keep), or going over the charge with the inquisitor's clerk, who swore to it.
- **Endings.**
  - *Freed* (the crowd at the gate; the house lets her go quietly): won by persuasion; who pays: `{digger}` (named to the house; his parish shuns him). ← shown
  - *Captive stays by choice* (she recites what she read of the roll before the Mount at the feast): won by nobody; who pays: the heart (banished from First Peoples land; `{home_hall}` loses its master) · the Mount's name.
  - *Burn your copy* (code-break: her page burned; freed for want of proof): won by stealth; who pays: the record (the roll's words now live only in memory) · the heart (she promises never to write them).
- **A month on (Freed).** Teaches again, and writes down what she remembers for the leader to read when she is healed / the dig stays closed / the inquisitor says the roll was a heretic's forgery.
