### Branch a × E1 · Delivered

*Blow a: player · framed · for destroying a text (`framed-damage`) → door Investigate Sabotage. Ending shown: Delivered.*

- **R1 · `{lodge_hall}`.** At `{lodge_hall}`, the hall nearest the camp, the heart's mother is mocked as "the surveyor who walked into the green for nothing" (Restore *Lost good name*), and the heart, her son, who can't read, wants her journal read to him (his want), and says he found it in her hut at the camp the day she didn't come back and has kept it since, wrapped in her scarf (his past), while the journal, {journal_look}, is "a journal in her own cipher, which nobody at the hall can read".
- **R2 · `{widow_town}`.** At `{widow_town}` a surveyor's widow means to burn her husband's field journals, told they carry the green, and the player copies them before they burn (Acquire *Copy it before it burns*), "field journals burned as green-touched", while in secret the captain's sergeant who leads the escorts (the Hand) told her so, as he tells the camps the green's too near to walk alone, because they are twenty years of the true edge on the captain's stretch, the last record outside the Wayhouse his redrawn lines could be checked against.
- **R3 · `{r3_hall}`.** At `{r3_hall}` the year's copy of the Wayhouse's edge-map register, made each season from the halls' lodgings, has a line altered by its clerk for himself (Investigate *The ledger doesn't add up*), and shows ∅ "the register's 'carried by' column, blank since the escorts began".
- **Reveal · `{lantern_hall}`.** At `{lantern_hall}`, where the heart brings the player to ask the Lantern captain who escorted his mother's camp, the Lantern contract camp that used to escort the surveyors quarrels with the Wardens who took the contract (Dispute *Broken agreement*), and the Lantern captain says he saw the Wardens' escort redraw journals by a tower fire on the road from the camp, the season they took the contract, while the heart adds, from his mother's last post, that it is the captain's stretch, where she was sent in, and the Lantern captain adds the old debt, from his chapter's own contract book, that the Lantern escorted the Wayhouse's certifiers a generation ago and the book records where a lord's steward had them set the towers' line.
  The Lantern captain asks the player three things: who at the hall can read a journal "in her own cipher, which nobody at the hall can read", why a widow would burn "field journals burned as green-touched", and who has signed "the register's 'carried by' column" since the escorts began.
- **Chapter 1's move.** The captain closes the survey camp "for its safety" and keeps its journals.
- **C1-1 · `{watchtower}`.** The player goes up to the watchtower above the closed camp to watch where the journals went (Investigate *Watch them*), and sees "a straight-edge and a pot of camp ink in a Warden tower".
- **C1-2 · `{true_edge}`.** Therefore the player takes the edge's readings again with the camp's staff and chain (Acquire *Take the readings*), and finds "the true edge, half a day's walk out from the redrawn line".
- **C1-3 · `{green_wall}`.** But at the Green Wall the sergeant (the Hand) cries the heart's mother a deserter who walked into the green, the son struck him, and he demands redress (Dispute *Public offense*), while her brother, a sergeant of the Wall, stands with the Hand, and an old surveyor who learned her cipher on the surveys they walked together, and left the camps after a quarrel with the captain, takes her journal from the heart to read at last, and will help for his own old journals lodged at the Wayhouse under his name.
- **C2-0 · `{edge_village_b}`.** At pressure two, at `{edge_village_b}`, the closed camp's surveyors, now "green-touched", are barred from its inn by its headman (Passage *Unwelcome cargo*).
- **Blow and door (a) · `{green_wall}`.** At the Green Wall a camp journal is "lost" from the escort's packs, and the player, who walked the true edge with the camp's staff, is framed for it and must find who took it (Investigate *Sabotage*), and on the door the old surveyor, who knows the camps' calendar, says the stewards vote the levy at `{lodge_hall}` on `{vote_day}`, on the season's lodged maps: "the vote on `{vote_day}`".
- **Code-bending way.** The door carries one extra way that destroys a text: cut the sergeant's redrawn page out of one lodged journal.
- **Crisis · at `{hut}`.** The redrawn journals are lodged at `{lodge_hall}`, the stewards vote there on `{vote_day}`, `{days}` days off, the captain's towers bar green-touched cargo from his stretch, and at the old surveyor's hut beyond the towers, {hut_look}, where the true readings wait with his mother's journal, read to him at last, the heart gives the card for her journal and the vote (Passage *Samples from the green*, the captain's towers as the route's authority): carry the truth through the line, and say what to let be read on the way.
  - *How it reads blow a:* With the player framed for a journal lost from the escort's packs, the captain's towers search the player first, so the journals must cross the tower line in other hands.
  - *Standing:* Framed for the lost journal, the player keeps rank but no Warden tower will pass the player's pack unsearched, so the player travels as the old surveyor's companion, carrying nothing of the journals.
  - *What the player holds:* Knowing from the Lantern captain that the journals are redrawn is not the true journals, a redrawing he saw years ago is no reading, the true readings the player took, the widow's journals the player copied and the mother's journal are at the hut and not at the hall, the captain's towers stand between, and no Writer can make the stewards wait, so the crisis is that carriage.
  - *The tell:* The last page is in a different, hurried hand, for it names the captain's order sending her in.
  - *The ways:* The ways are burning the redrawn journals lodged at `{lodge_hall}`, so the stewards have nothing to vote on (the code-break), the old surveyor walking it along the tower line by a Warden-wise route (his price: his journals lodged), or sealing it under a copyist's wax, as the hall's letters.
