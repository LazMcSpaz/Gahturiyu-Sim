# W6-A · The Copies Sent for Mending

Every branch × ending combination of `spines.md` draft 5, rendered by `roll_samples.py --render-all`. Slots are shown as `{slot}`, flavour as its own slot; one sentence per line. The routine's swaps are not multiplied out (each is one card's other premise, printed in the spine's field 18).

**8 combinations:** 2 branch(es) × 4 endings (three, and the seat ending).

### Branch a × E1 · Delivered

*Blow a: player · framed · for destroying a text (`framed-damage`) → door Investigate Sabotage. Ending shown: Delivered.*

- **R1 · `{court}`.** At the seat of `{court}` the lord's archivist won't lend the halls his master's court roll (Acquire *Won't sell*), for it is a short roll, kept without the margin grants as a court's always is, and the last full copy his house sent for mending never came back: ∅ "a copy sent for mending that never came back".
- **R2 · `{wayhouse}`.** At the Wayhouse, where she copies the land rolls by night for mending, the heart disputes her night-copy pay with her copy master (Dispute *Broken agreement*), and the heart, an apprentice who copies the old imperial hand by shape, tells the player she wants to be taught to read what she copies (her want), that she came to the halls a foundling from `{valley}` (her past), and that she keeps "the shape-sheets under her bed", every page she has copied, the valley's page on top, while the copies are made by the leader's letter, which the eldest copyist reads out to the night table: "copies made for mending, of rolls that weren't torn".
- **R3 · `{reading_room}`.** At `{reading_room}` the halls' mending book doesn't match the hall's shelves (Investigate *The ledger doesn't add up*), showing "the courier's mark against 'returned', in one ink for a year", while in secret the leader's courier (the Hand) signs every copy back in as returned to its hall, so no hall asks after a copy sitting unread at the tower, and the copies go astray one at a time without anyone counting.
- **Reveal · `{reading_room}`.** At `{reading_room}` a pedlar sells a roll-case bearing the hall's mark, bought from a carter who found it fallen from the courier's mule on the road out (Acquire *Passed on*), and the old reader who taught the leader her letters buys it and reads it with the player, and its mending label, in the courier's hand with the "returned" mark he signs, names no hall to mend a roll that was never torn, while the old reader adds, from the hall's file of mending orders, that every order is in the leader's hand, which he taught her, and from the letter she wrote him last winter that her reason is a Sharp Hands buyer waiting (the counting-house traders who buy up claims), who would buy the valley from whoever the court favoured, and she means the court to favour no one he can buy from.
  The label says the plants in its own marks: "returned", in the courier's one ink, "the courier's mark against 'returned', in one ink for a year", a roll sent for mending that was never torn, "copies made for mending, of rolls that weren't torn", and no hall to send it back to, "a copy sent for mending that never came back".
- **Chapter 1's move.** By the leader's letter the master of `{reading_room}` takes the reading room's master copy of the land rolls into his own keeping, "to be sent for mending".
- **C1-1 · `{tower}`.** The player follows the courier's road by the tolls he paid to a lone tower, {tower_look}, whose keeper, a pensioned copyist, keeps what the courier leaves without knowing what it is worth (Acquire *Unknown worth*), and finds "shelves of copies, dry and unread".
- **C1-2 · `{reading_room}`.** Therefore at `{reading_room}` the player watches the hall's strongroom, where the master copy waits under the leader's letter (Investigate *Watch them*), and learns of "the courier due at the new moon", while the hall's master, ashamed of the letter, says he is "a master who'd sooner copy than send", and would take a copy into his hall if one came.
- **C1-3 · `{reading_room}`.** But the new moon has come and the courier (the Hand) has taken the master copy out under the leader's letter, and when the ashamed hall master cries him a thief before the hall the courier demands redress with the letter in his hand (Dispute *Public offense*), while the valley's headwoman, who needs a copy at the court for her claim to the upper fields, says the margin grants name the families who held the valley before the claim, that a foundling of the valley might be of one of them, and that she will help for her claim copied free.
- **C2-0 · `{wayhouse}`.** At pressure two, at the Wayhouse, the leader's letter puts the Wayhouse's own land rolls under her seal "until the mending is done", and no night copyist may touch them: "the land rolls under her seal" (Acquire *Under seal*).
- **Blow and door (a) · `{reading_room}`.** At `{reading_room}` the player, seen on the courier's road following his tolls to the tower, is framed for the lost master copy, whose roll-case is found in the ditch, and must find who lost it (Investigate *Sabotage*), and on the door the heart, who has watched them come and go every night, writes to the player by the Wall's night post that the carters who bring the Wayhouse its paper are never searched at its door: "the paper carters, never searched".
- **Code-bending way.** The door carries one extra way that takes a side in public: sign a private letter vouching for the claimants' copy.
- **Crisis · at `{wayhouse}`, acted at `{court}`.** The master copy has been "lost" on the road, the court of `{court}` sits on the valley's claims in `{days}` days and asks the Writers for the full roll, margins and all, and the leader's letter orders the court's copy made without the margin grants and forbids any copy of the land rolls to leave the Wayhouse without her seal, while the courier, {courier_manner}, keeps the door and the heart holds a full copy in her shape-sheets, so the eldest copyist, who must read the order aloud and hates it, gives the card (Passage *Smuggling*, the leader's order as the route's authority): obey the head of the guild, or carry the copy out.
  - *How it reads blow a:* With the player framed for the lost master copy, the courier at the door has the leader's word to search the player first, so whatever goes out must go out in other hands.
  - *Standing:* Framed for the lost master copy and not yet cleared, the player keeps rank but is barred by the leader's letter from the room where the land rolls lie, and comes to the Wayhouse's door as the heart's companion, not as a copyist of the rolls.
  - *What the player holds:* The player knows the leader is the enemy from the roll-case's label and has seen the tower's shelves, the old reader holds her own letter giving her reason, and the heart holds a full copy, but no Writer outranks the head of the guild, her order is lawful in the halls, a letter to her old teacher is private and no hall acts against its head on it, the tower shows her losses and not that the court's copy is short, and the heart's copy is inside the Wayhouse under her order, so the payload lands unless the copy gets out, and the crisis is that chance.
  - *The tell:* She asks to carry the copy herself, though she can't read it, and, unknown to her, it carries her own family's line in the valley's margin grants.
  - *The ways:* The ways are taking a side in public, the copyists carrying it out openly before the town (the code-break), the valley's headwoman, come to Highcrossing for her copy, carrying it out in her own satchel (her price: her claim copied free), or hiding it inside ordinary goods, in the paper carters' empty bales.
- **Endings.**
  - *Delivered* (the full copy reaches `{court}`; the claims are heard on both): won by stealth; who pays: the heart (struck off the roll by the leader's letter) · `{eldest}` (put out for letting her pass). ← shown
  - *A copy in every hall* (copied into every hall; no one place can hold the only roll again): won by persuasion; who pays: the guild's hold on land (with a copy in every hall, no one reading of the rolls can decide a claim again) · the courier (named).
  - *Seized* (the courier takes it at the door): won by nobody; who pays: the valley's holders (they lose) · the heart (sent to a far hall).
  - *take the road* (the player takes the leader's road and her seat): won by persuasion; who pays: the leader (her seat) · the player (the road, always).
- **A month on (Delivered).** Taught to read at last, in the valley / the valley's holders keep their land, and the master copy's loss is laid on the courier, not the player / the leader writes, and begins again elsewhere.

- **C2-0 · `{wayhouse}`.** At pressure two, at the Wayhouse, the leader's letter puts the Wayhouse's own land rolls under her seal "until the mending is done", and no night copyist may touch them: "the land rolls under her seal" (Acquire *Under seal*).
- **Blow and door (b) · `{wayhouse}`.** At the Wayhouse the heart is found copying its roll without leave, for the claimants, and the leader's letter has her cried down for taking a side (Restore *Lost good name*, cause *exaggerated*: she copied by shape, for no side), and on the door she writes to the player by the Wall's night post, for she has watched them come and go every night, that the carters who bring the Wayhouse its paper are never searched at its door: "the paper carters, never searched".
- **Code-bending way.** The door carries one extra way that takes a side in public: sign a private letter vouching for the claimants' copy.
- **Crisis · at `{wayhouse}`, acted at `{court}`.** The master copy has been "lost" on the road, the court of `{court}` sits on the valley's claims in `{days}` days and asks the Writers for the full roll, margins and all, and the leader's letter orders the court's copy made without the margin grants and forbids any copy of the land rolls to leave the Wayhouse without her seal, while the courier, {courier_manner}, keeps the door and the heart holds a full copy in her shape-sheets, so the eldest copyist, who must read the order aloud and hates it, gives the card (Passage *Smuggling*, the leader's order as the route's authority): obey the head of the guild, or carry the copy out.

  - *What the player holds:* The player knows the leader is the enemy from the roll-case's label and has seen the tower's shelves, the old reader holds her own letter giving her reason, and the heart holds a full copy, but no Writer outranks the head of the guild, her order is lawful in the halls, a letter to her old teacher is private and no hall acts against its head on it, the tower shows her losses and not that the court's copy is short, and the heart's copy is inside the Wayhouse under her order, so the payload lands unless the copy gets out, and the crisis is that chance.
