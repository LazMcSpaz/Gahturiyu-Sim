### Branch a × E1 · Amends made

*Blow a: guild · hall seized · shut by her own order (`seized-order`) → door Acquire Under seal. Ending shown: Amends made.*

- **R1 · `{reading_room}`.** At `{reading_room}` a will has been altered for the one who profits (Investigate *The ledger doesn't add up*), and the heart, the old clerk who knows the hall's copying book, who copied what and when, tells the player she would give anything to copy an archive whole and wants to copy the archive of `{lord}` herself (her want), and that she wrote to the leader twice asking leave to copy it and was told to wait (her past), while on the book's facing page is "a commission entered 'held, by letter', three seasons running".
- **R2 · `{lord}`.** At the seat of `{lord}` the steward's clerk means to burn the steward's letter-book, and the player copies it before it burns (Acquire *Copy it before it burns*), "a steward's letter-book, three letters to the halls in a year", while in secret the leader's courier (the Hand), who carried each "held" answer, told the clerk that begging letters shame his master and the halls keep copies anyway, because the letter-book is the one record outside the halls that the copying was asked for, and burned, it lets the halls say it was never pressed.
- **R3 · `{r3_hall}`.** At `{r3_hall}` two heirs dispute their father's will, which burned in a house fire (Dispute *Contested inheritance*), and the clue is an absence: ∅ "a will the halls never got round to copying".
- **Reveal · `{reading_room}`.** At `{reading_room}` the steward of `{lord}`, who rides in from the seat and is laughed at as the man who "begs the halls" (Restore *Lost good name*), is the knower, and the hall's old reader gives what he shows, the steward's own copies of his three requests asking the halls to copy his master's archive and the hall's copying book, where each is entered "held, by letter" a week after it came, and adds whose letter held them, from the hall's letter file, the leader's hand, which he taught her, and why, from what she wrote him when he asked: the weaker heir would sell to the Sharp Hands, the counting-house traders who buy up land, so she would choose the lord's heir by the halls' roll.
  The old reader lays each of the steward's requests beside its line in the copying book, "held, by letter, three seasons running", the steward's letter-book count beside them, "three letters to the halls in a year", and the burned will beside both, "a will the halls never got round to copying", which is what an archive left uncopied comes to.
- **Chapter 1's move.** By the leader's letter the hall master of `{reading_room}` dismisses before the hall the copyist who asked who has been holding the commission for the archive of `{lord}`.
- **C1-1 · `{lord}`.** The player rides out to the archive tower at the seat of `{lord}`, which the steward wants made safe before winter and the heirs won't pay for (Restore *Broken works*), and climbs "the archive's lamp-lit stair, oil in every niche", {stair_look}.
- **C1-2 · `{lord}`.** But the move reaches the seat: in the seat's hall the leader's courier (the Hand) holds the commission's seal and won't release it without the leader's word (Acquire *Won't sell*), and a copy a court honours in a succession is a halls' copy under that seal, for the steward's own pens would make only a steward's copy, which each heir would call forged.
- **C1-3 · `{lord}`.** Therefore, with no seal, the steward watches the archive door himself (Investigate *Watch them*), and sees "two heirs' men at one door", the old lord's son's and his niece's, and the steward, who begged for the copying because he loves the old lord, asks the player to watch with him and to say in public, when it comes to it, that he asked, and that is his price.
- **C2-0 · `{lord}`.** At pressure two, at the seat, the old lord's ward is to be moved out before the heirs come to blows, and won't go (Passage *Unwilling passenger*).
- **Blow and door (a) · `{reading_room}`.** By the leader's letter the copying book of `{reading_room}` is sealed "while the succession is argued", and the player must get at it under the seal (Acquire *Under seal*), and on the door the heart shows the season's page in her own working book, in her own hand, with the leader's "held" letters pinned to it: "the page in her own hand".
- **Code-bending way.** The door carries one extra way that takes a side in public: tell one heir, privately, what the halls' roll says.
- **Crisis · at `{reading_room}`.** The old lord has died, both heirs' men have fought at the archive door, a lamp has gone over on the oiled stair and the archive has burned uncopied, and the heirs have come to the halls for the only roll, so a letter of the leader's that waited sealed in the strongroom of `{reading_room}` "to be opened if the archive is lost", "dated the week before the fire", must be read before the whole hall, naming the heart as the clerk who "lost" the steward's requests and sending her to a far hall, and the old reader who taught the leader her letters, {tutor_manner}, gives the card because he must read it aloud (Reckoning *Betrayal*, the leader as wrongdoer, the demand a confession): answer the letter with what the hall holds and split the hall masters over their head, or let the heart go and the only roll be read the leader's way.
  - *How it reads blow a:* With the copying book sealed while the succession is argued, the page in her own working book is the only one the hall can be shown, and it is the accused clerk's own.
  - *What the player holds:* The page in the heart's own hand, the old reader's letter from the leader giving her reason and the steward's own copies of his three requests already show who held the commission, and the player holds the copied letter-book, but the fire was nobody's to stop, for without the commission's seal no halls' copy could be made and the heirs' men at one door were the heirs', and a hall reads its head's sealed letter as an order, so answering it with her own private letter and a clerk's page means a hall standing against its leader in public, and the crisis is the price of that, not the doubt.
  - *The tell:* The courier's saddlebag is stuffed with old wrappers he never throws away, for he carried every "held" letter and kept the wrappers.
  - *The ways:* The ways are taking a side in public, the halls backing the heir the steward says the archive favoured (the code-break), the steward, come in with the heirs, swearing he wrote three times and was answered "held, by letter" (his price: its being said in public), or bringing the betrayer to the betrayed, the leader coming off the road to `{reading_room}` to meet the heart face to face.
- **Endings.**
  - *Amends made* (face to face, the leader takes back her letter and lets the heart copy what's left): won by persuasion; who pays: the leader (her word, before the hall masters) · the steward (named as having begged). ← shown
  - *Open the ledgers* (the copying book read at every hall): won by law; who pays: the guild (the hall masters split over her) · the leader.
  - *Take a side at last* (code-break: the halls back the heir the steward says the archive favoured, in public): won by law; who pays: the guild's neutrality · the other heir's tenants.
  - *take the road* (the player takes the leader's road and her seat): won by persuasion; who pays: the leader (her seat) · the player (the road, always).
- **A month on (Amends made).** Copies the archive's charred remains, which is her want, smaller, and her working book goes back on the hall's shelf / the succession is argued from scraps / the leader walks on, watched.
