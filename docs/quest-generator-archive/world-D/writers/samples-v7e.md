- **Tell** · The key-book, in the heart's own hand, reads "one truce, received sealed, in our hand", for what the strongroom holds is the hall's door copy, and the steward took the sealed original away with his objection.
- **Held** · The player saw the back-stair satchel and knows the hold rule before the last letter goes, the heart holds the hall's door copy of the truce, and the leader has read the retired steward's letters, but the letter is a true debt carried unsigned by the Wall's own post, no rule lets a Writer open a sealed letter or warn one lord of another's debt, the old letters prove the sending and not that the debt is false, a lodging takes the sealed truce and not a clerk's copy, and a ruling reads the roll's page and nobody's memory, so the hold lands, and the crisis is the player's chance against the hold itself.
- **E1 · Lodged on the ruling** · The ruling clears the debt by the grinding, the steward withdraws and the truce is lodged, and the steward's clerk pays (named, and her master turns her off).
- **E2 · Burn your copy** · The ford-mill page of the loan roll is burned, so the objection has no debt to rest on and the steward must hand the sealed truce back for its lodging, and the heart pays (the line she copied as a girl is ash, what she remembers of it rules nothing, and she leaves the rolls), as does Southroad's house (a true claim gone).
- **E3 · Keep the balance** · The hold stands and the lodging day passes, and the ford's tenants pay (the race is broken again within the season), as does the steward's clerk.

**A month on (E1, branch b)** · The old clerk keeps the rolls and has taken an apprentice, and the player's stain waits on the hall masters' next table; the mill grinds for both banks; the master writes these lords no more letters, and waits.
