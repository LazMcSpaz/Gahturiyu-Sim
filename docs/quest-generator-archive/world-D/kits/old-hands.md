# The Old Hands: story kit (v6c draft)

*Replaces the **Story kit** subsection of `quest-guild-kits.md` §3 (The Old Hands). The header and the Profile above it are unchanged. Built to `test/world/fixlist-v6c.md` and `faction-stories.md` §10, §11 and §11a (v6c). New material is marked (Claude). *Tone kept: the native, old-family syndicate. It is quiet and coded, it serves the houses, it is rotting as the houses use it less, and it hates outsiders. Its places are the Old Gate House, back rooms, caches, quiet crossings and safehouse estates.*

**The paired feud** *(Claude; paired worlds only, about 1 in 3)*. This section is word for word the same in the Night Market kit. The planner rolls it once, after the pair's two enemies, and fixes it with the truce facts (§10, *One truth for a shared move*). The two enemies are this kit's OH2 or OH9, and the Night Market kit's N1 or N8.
1. **Who struck first:** the Market's side (this story's enemy) or the Old Hands' side (the Night Market story's enemy). The first striker's chapter-1 move is the feud's first public blow, and the other enemy's chapter-1 move answers it. Each story's chapter 2 grows from the other story's move (§10).
2. **Over what.** It must fit the first striker's scheme:

   | # | Over what | First striker may be |
   |---|---|---|
   | F1 | a contested site: a tavern both syndicates use, or a warehouse both claim (`faction-places.md` §5b, *Turf*) | any of OH2, OH9, N1, N8 |
   | F2 | a quiet crossing the Market's crews began using without leave | OH9 |
   | F3 | an old house that began buying from the Market | OH2, N1 |
   | F4 | a forger who changed sides | OH2, N8 |

3. **Who called the truce** (always at the contested site): *T1* the old don, by letter · *T2* the leader, over a card table · *T3* the lord of the town where the contested site stands, for the peace of his streets · *T4* the carters' foreman whose crews haul for both. T3 and T4 are background people in authority-only slots (rulebook, *Background people in truths*), never cast.
4. **How it ended** (the shared truce letter): *a* a knife at the truce table (`men` or `craft`) · *b* a fall from the crossing's bridge (`men` or `inside`) · *c* a poisoned cup (`inside` or `craft`). **Both killers' means must meet it**, so under OH2 (`coin` `inside`) the truce ends only by *b* or *c*.
5. **Who holds *Blood debt*:** one story takes its kit's *Blood debt* (Old Hands C2, Night Market C1), and the other takes its non-Reckoning paired crisis (Old Hands C5, Night Market C2). The roll is made among what the stakes allow: C5 decides only S1, so an Old Hands story whose stake is S3 or S6 holds *Blood debt*.
