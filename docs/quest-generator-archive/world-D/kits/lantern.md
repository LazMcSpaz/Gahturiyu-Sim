# The Lantern Company: story kit (v6c draft)

A **complete replacement** for `guild-kits.md` §2 *Story kit* (the Profile, signature premises, twist rows and faction twists above it don't change). Built to `test/world/fixlist-v6c.md` and faction-stories §11 and §11a (v6c). Everything new is marked *(Claude)*; entries kept from v5.1 keep their ids.

**What changed from v5.1, in short:**
- 8 enemies (L6, L7, L8 new), 6 stakes (S6 new), 5 hearts (H5 new), a chapter-1 move table (9 moves), 5 odd jobs with five thread kinds (O4, O5 new), 8 crises (C5–C8 new; 3 of 8 Reckoning, down from 3 of 4), and an ending pool of 6.
- **No crisis lists its enemies.** A part is legal when the enemy's means meet its `needs`, the stake is on its list, and the shape and want filters pass. The only structural exceptions are Schism (a *Within* enemy) and Betrayal (a *Within* enemy, for the premise's working link to the heart).
- **Each enemy names the stakes its scheme reaches** *(Claude)*, so every story is about one thing (throughline point 3).
- The old C1 (the chapter held in Lanternhall's yard) came up 70% of the time, because L1, L3 and L5 all reached it and the other crises were each one enemy's. C1 now competes with 2–3 other crises under every enemy that reaches it.

**Reading the tags** (faction-stories §11a): as in the Writers kit. `needs` is any one of the listed means ("A **and** B" needs both); `uses`, the means the roll records, picks **`paper` last** *(Claude)*; a part marked *writ* counts toward writ fatigue when its `uses` is `paper`, and **a Lantern story rolls at most one writ part** *(Claude)*. Shape filters: *W* = *Within* only; *not W* = never *Within*; *Beside* = *Beside* only. **Under *the cost*** (L5, L8): only moves marked *cost: yes*, and never crises C3 or C7, which would make the harm a plan (faction-stories §11a point 5).

### Throughline test *(Claude)*

**Method.** `test/world/D/kits/throughline.py` holds this kit as data, as for the Writers. It lists every legal combination of enemy, stake, chapter-1 move, chapter-2 blow and crisis, under the needs, stake lists, shape and want filters, *the cost*'s rules, the blow rules above, at least 3 fitting endings and at most one writ part. **Result:** 413 legal combinations, 19 legal enemy + crisis pairs; every enemy reaches at least 2 crises and at least 2 moves.

**What the test caught, and the fixes** (all made above):
- *The same act twice.* A recruiter's or factor's move "called in the debts", and then the crisis called them in again. The move now **buys** the debts in public, and the crisis calls them.
