# The Writers: story kit (v6c draft)

A **complete replacement** for `guild-kits.md` §1 *Story kit* (the Profile, signature premises, twist rows and faction twists above it don't change). Built to `test/world/fixlist-v6c.md` and faction-stories §11 and §11a (v6c). Everything new is marked *(Claude)*; entries kept from v5.1 keep their ids.

**What changed from v5.1, in short:**
- 8 enemies (W7, W8 new), 7 stakes (S6, S7 new), 5 hearts (H5 new), a chapter-1 move table (9 moves), 5 odd jobs with five thread kinds (O4, O5 new), 8 crises (C5–C8 new; 3 of 8 Reckoning, down from 3 of 4), and an ending pool of 6 plus the seat ending.
- **No crisis lists its enemies.** A part is legal when the enemy's means meet its `needs`, the stake is on its list, and the shape and want filters pass. The old exceptions that stay are structural: Schism needs a *Within* enemy who isn't the leader; Betrayal needs a *Within* enemy, because the premise needs a working link to the heart.
- **Each enemy names the stakes its scheme reaches** *(Claude)*. Tags alone let a forger threaten the survey rolls; the list keeps every story about one thing (throughline point 3).
- The old C1 was rolled in three of four sample worlds, because W1, W4 and W6 had nothing else. Now every enemy has 2–3 crises (W6, the leader, has 2).

**Reading the tags** (faction-stories §11a):
- `needs`: any one of the listed means, among the enemy's, is enough. "A **and** B" needs both.
- `uses`: the means the roll records as carrying the part out. Where several fit, the keyed roll picks one; **`paper` is picked last** *(Claude)*, so writ fatigue is spent only when nothing else will do.
- **Writ**: a part marked *writ* counts toward writ fatigue (faction-stories §9) when its `uses` is `paper`. **A Writers story rolls at most one writ part** *(Claude)*: the world allows 2, and one story shouldn't spend both.
- **Shape** filters: *W* = *Within* only; *not W* = never *Within*; *Above* = *Above* only.
- **Under *the cost*** (W1, W6): only moves marked *cost: yes* (the enemy protecting what pays them, faction-stories §11a point 5).
