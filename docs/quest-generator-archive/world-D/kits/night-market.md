# The Night Market: story kit (v6c draft)

*Replaces the **Story kit** subsection of `quest-guild-kits.md` §4 (The Night Market). The header and the Profile above it are unchanged. Built to `test/world/fixlist-v6c.md` and `faction-stories.md` §10, §11 and §11a (v6c). New material is marked (Claude). *Tone kept: the open syndicate. It is mixed and slick, anyone can join, and it runs on the markets under roads, the coves, the toll-dodge trails, forged notes and the shared take. Its code is to share the take and never inform. Its leader wants the biggest score ever, and to stay free.*

**The paired feud** *(Claude; paired worlds only, about 1 in 3)*. This section is word for word the same in the Old Hands kit. The planner rolls it once, after the pair's two enemies, and fixes it with the truce facts (§10, *One truth for a shared move*). The two enemies are the Old Hands kit's OH2 or OH9, and this kit's N1 or N8.
1. **Who struck first:** the Market's side (the Old Hands story's enemy) or the Old Hands' side (this story's enemy). The first striker's chapter-1 move is the feud's first public blow, and the other enemy's chapter-1 move answers it. Each story's chapter 2 grows from the other story's move (§10).
2. **Over what.** It must fit the first striker's scheme:

   | # | Over what | First striker may be |
   |---|---|---|
   | F1 | a contested site: a tavern both syndicates use, or a warehouse both claim (`faction-places.md` §5b, *Turf*) | any of OH2, OH9, N1, N8 |
   | F2 | a quiet crossing the Market's crews began using without leave | OH9 |
   | F3 | an old house that began buying from the Market | OH2, N1 |
   | F4 | a forger who changed sides | OH2, N8 |

3. **Who called the truce** (always at the contested site): *T1* the old don, by letter · *T2* the leader, over a card table · *T3* the lord of the town where the contested site stands, for the peace of his streets · *T4* the carters' foreman whose crews haul for both. T3 and T4 are background people in authority-only slots (rulebook, *Background people in truths*), never cast.
4. **How it ended** (the shared truce letter): *a* a knife at the truce table (`men` or `craft`) · *b* a fall from the crossing's bridge (`men` or `inside`) · *c* a poisoned cup (`inside` or `craft`). **Both killers' means must meet it**, so under OH2 (`coin` `inside`) the truce ends only by *b* or *c*.
5. **Who holds *Blood debt*:** one story takes its kit's *Blood debt* (Old Hands C2, Night Market C1), and the other takes its non-Reckoning paired crisis (Old Hands C5, Night Market C2). The roll is made among what the stakes allow: C2 decides only S1, so a Night Market story whose stake is S2 or S5 holds *Blood debt*.

*Variety it adds:* 2 × 2 enemy pairs, which side holds *Blood debt*, four causes, two first strikers, four callers and three letters, filtered by fit. That gives 88 legal feuds before the callers multiply them, where v5.1 had one feud told one way three letters at a time.

**Compatibility:**
- **N1 and N8 are rolled only in paired mode,** and paired mode always rolls one of them. Both are *Beside*, from the Old Hands, and each is the killer of this story's dead (§10). In a paired world this story's crisis is C1 or C2 (paired), and the feud above is rolled once for both stories.
- **An Old Hands enemy who uses the watch or the toll men** (N1 riding with the raid, N8 showing a door) **breaks his own syndicate's code.** That is the Old Hands' rot (`world-factions.md`, *Depth*: "they're getting desperate, so more reckless"). A phase-3 card may say so; nothing else changes.
- **N5** takes C3 only, never Schism. *Take the shop* is its seat ending, a fourth beside the 3 rolled.
- **N9** takes S1 or S4 only (his scheme is the coves).
- **N7** reaches only S1 (through the way in, which the carters know) and S5. His crises are C2 (the raid on his word) and C6 (the trail race).
- **C3 (Betrayal)** needs an employer link to the heart, which the cast step makes under *Within*.
- **C5:** *The score*'s twist rows apply (never *meant for destruction*). The holding place is guarded, so the drain way is removed if it is fortified.
- **C6:** terms are never blood, and the eldest fence is ring-keeper. A trail sworn to the race is a matter already sworn, as *Champion's bout* requires.
- **C8 (Schism)** needs a *Within* enemy other than the leader (N2). Its *disown quietly* way never rolls, because the Market has no hidden side to keep silent about.
- **One enemy side per faction:** N2 and N5 make the Night Market the enemy's side, so Writers W3 and Dueling D4 are barred with them. N3 is barred when another story's enemy is a Sharp Hands member (Old Hands OH8, Lantern L8). N4's toll lord is cast from a lord group no other story uses as its enemy side (not the Free Lords under Writers W2 or Lantern L1, not the Banner Lords under Dueling D7). N6 is barred when the Lantern Company is another story's enemy side (a *Within* Lantern enemy: L2, L3). N7 counts as the Road Crews and N9 as the Tide Houses.
