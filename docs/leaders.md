# Faction leaders (Laz, 2026-10-12)
The faces the player can talk to: the leader of each faction and of each faction within a major. Chosen by Laz from options; Claude's additions are marked *(Claude)*. Names are in the table below.

## Names (chosen by Laz, 2026-10-12)
Given names come from the naming module's lists (`assets/lang/names/`), so they follow each people's sound rules. Full names follow each culture's pattern: Roduro add their house, Qotiro their latest deed, Horaro their mother's line or sea-place, Ṭaḍoro their teacher or road. **The bynames are Claude's suggestions** (change freely).

## The First Peoples

### Seated Houses: the blind archivist-lord
- **Who:** a Roduro elder lord who has gone blind and rules from memory alone.
- **Traits:**
  - **secretly tired:** he knows the Alliance is dying and is exhausted from holding it up;
  - **sharp memory:** remembers every slight, debt and name, for generations;
  - **sometimes cruel and ruthless,** even though he knows it's essentially over for his people.
- **Goal:** keep the Tide Houses, and stop more Horaro leaving for the Far Sails.

### True Line: mother and son
- **Who:** a Roduro mother and son. She schemes; he's the face they want on the seat.
- **Traits:**
  - **charismatic:** draws the young and the bitter to the cause;
  - **willing to deal:** will ally with anyone, even the Holy House or the Binders, to win.
- **Goal:** purify the Alliance: cut the Horaro out entirely, Roduro only.

### Tide Houses: the season's speaker
- **Who:** a Horaro woman whose rotating turn as speaker landed in a crisis.
- **Traits:**
  - **warm and indirect:** never says no outright; everything comes through stories;
  - **divided loyalties:** loves the Alliance and resents it in equal measure.
- **Goal:** bring the Far Sails home, reconciling the Horaro who left.
- *(Claude)* Her term ends with the season, so she's racing the clock, and the next speaker might not share her aim.

## The Great House

### The Throne: the emperor's sister
- **Who:** a Roduro woman who holds the court together while her brother, the emperor, fades.
- **Traits:**
  - **masterful liar:** hides the emperor's weakness behind ceremony;
  - **lonely:** no one at court can be trusted; she aches for a real ally.
- **Goals:**
  - hide the emperor's weakness;
  - secure the succession, so the Throne survives him.

### Banner Lords: two leading lords
- **Who:** two lords lead the loyal camp, side by side and in competition:
  - **the old general:** a Roduro veteran of the last real imperial war, loyal to the bone;
  - **the Ṭaḍoro-born lord:** a rare Ṭaḍoro raised to lordship for service, and despised by old blood.
- **Traits** (shared between them; *(Claude)* suggested split in brackets):
  - **social climber:** loyalty is a ladder (mostly the Ṭaḍoro lord);
  - **suspects the truth:** has begun to guess the emperor is weak (mostly the general, who knew him in his prime);
  - **warm to commoners:** their people actually love them (both).
- **Goals:**
  - defend the Throne: protect the emperor's sister and the court;
  - earn the succession: be named heir or regent if the emperor dies. *(Claude)* That puts the two of them on a collision course, and the emperor's sister has to choose.

### Free Lords: the Qotiro-blooded lord
- **Who:** a half-Qotiro lord with ties to the Binders. Feared, and useful.
- **Traits:**
  - **secretly principled:** he defies the Throne because he thinks it's a lie, not out of greed;
  - **paranoid:** sees knives everywhere, and is often right.
- **Goal:** break from the Empire and make his lands an independent realm.
- *(Claude)* He's one of the few who knows (or guesses) how weak the emperor is, and thinks pretending otherwise is the real betrayal.

## The Open Hand

### Open Council: the freed bondswoman
- **Who:** a Horaro woman who bought her way out of bondage and built a trading house.
- **Traits:**
  - **inspiring speaker:** can fire up a crowd;
  - **losing grip:** feels the Sharp Hands taking over and can't stop it.
- **Goals:**
  - rein in the Sharp Hands and win back the soul of the faction;
  - free the bonded: buy out or break Binder contracts.

### Sharp Hands: the Ṭaḍoro broker and the merchant twins
- **Who:** a three-way partnership:
  - **a silver-tongued Ṭaḍoro broker** who controls the notes and the debts;
  - **Qotiro merchant twins:** one smiles and deals, one counts and threatens.
- **Traits:**
  - **flatterers:** they make everyone feel like a special friend;
  - **secret Binder ties:** they quietly profit from slave labour.
- **Goals:**
  - take the council: push out the freed bondswoman and run the Open Hand;
  - control the money: get a grip on the Ṭaḍoro exchange houses.
- *(Claude)* Their secret is the freed bondswoman's whole cause turned inside out. If it comes out, it could break them, or the Open Hand.

### Road Crews: the elected foreman
- **Who:** a Roduro carter the crews voted in. He hates the title.
- **Traits:**
  - **blunt:** says exactly what he thinks;
  - **cynical:** has heard the council's speeches too many times;
  - **knows every road:** every shortcut, ambush spot and safe camp.
- **Goals:**
  - fair pay: a real share, not whatever the Sharp Hands decide;
  - keep out of politics: he just wants to haul in peace (and politics keeps finding him).

## Minor factions

### The Holy House: two rivals
- **Who:**
  - **the high priest:** militant; he turned the church into an army;
  - **a sincere old priestess** who opposes him from within (the voice of the true believers).
- **Traits** (the high priest):
  - **lost his faith:** privately believes none of it any more;
  - **political:** faith is just leverage.
- **Goals** (the high priest):
  - win back believers, by sermon or by sword;
  - stay relevant: make the major powers need the church again.
- *(Claude)* The priestess wants the opposite of the same thing: win believers back by deserving them. She's a natural ally for a player who wants to reform the church rather than burn it.

### The Last Wave: the priestess and her war-chief
- **Who:** a Qotiro high priestess who rules (canon: priestesses rule), and her champion, a male war-chief, who fights for her.
- **Traits:**
  - **protective:** every child in the enclave is hers;
  - **pragmatic underneath:** she would make peace if it were offered with respect.
- **Goals:**
  - land of their own: a recognised homeland, by treaty or by force;
  - keep her people alive: food, walls, and the next winter.
- *(Claude)* The war-chief is the face outsiders meet first, and the one who tests them. Getting past him is how you reach her.

### The Far Sails: the fleet of equals
- **Who:** no single ruler. The captains vote, and the current speaker for the fleet is an easygoing old Horaro man.
- **Traits:** **homesick:** under the jokes he misses the family he left behind on the shore.
- **Goals:**
  - shelter runaways: make the fleet a home for the bonded and the outcast;
  - never go back: resist the Tide Houses' pleas to come home.
- *(Claude)* He and the Tide Houses' speaker want opposite things and miss each other's people. Their talks could be one of the game's quieter, sadder threads.

### The Binders: the ex-slave turned slaver
- **Who:** a Qotiro man who was once bonded himself, and now sells others.
- **Traits:**
  - **cold and predatory:** sizes up every stranger as stock;
  - **self-justifying:** can explain why every cruelty was necessary.
- **Goals:**
  - a title: to be made a true lord of the Great House;
  - crush the Open Hand: stop the freed bondswoman breaking his contracts.
- *(Claude)* He and the freed bondswoman both came out of bondage and chose opposite roads. Each is what the other could have become.

### The Wardens: the old married pair
- **Who:** a husband and wife who've held the same fort for thirty years.
- **Traits:**
  - **unbreakable:** never retreat, never beg, never lie to recruits;
  - **she knows more than she says:** she has seen things in the green that don't fit any story.
- **Goals:**
  - make the powers listen: get the major factions to send people and coin;
  - find the heart: they believe the Overgrowth has a centre that can be destroyed (and they're right; see `hidden-lore.md`).
- *(Claude)* He does the talking at the gates; she does the scouting in the green. What she's seen makes her the best lead toward the corpse at the heart.

### The Highfolk: the clan chieftain
- **Who:** a broad, laughing Roduro woman who leads the clans in the world.
- **Traits:**
  - **hospitable:** guests are sacred, once they've climbed;
  - **archaic and formal:** speaks like the oldest Roduro records.
- **Goals:**
  - stay free: no Great House banner in the mountains;
  - find a worthy outsider: someone who could carry the truth of the Womb and use it well. *(Claude)* That's the player's way in to the Womb: earn her trust.

### The Rooted: always a different face
- **Who:** no fixed leader. **The green speaks through a different person each time** you meet it: a sworn villager, a reanimated corpse, someone you knew. *(Claude)* Underneath, it's the wizard's corpse at the heart, using whoever is closest; the player shouldn't learn that until late.
- **Traits:**
  - **says "we":** there's no "I" left;
  - **flickers of self:** sometimes the old person breaks through, frightened;
  - **kind, terribly:** it genuinely wants to save you, by taking you.
- **Goals:**
  - cover the island;
  - protect the heart: keep anyone from reaching the corpse.
- *(Claude)* A nice cruelty: once the player has lost a squad member to the green, that person can be the face it uses next.

## Guilds

### The Writers: the wandering head
- **Who:** a Ṭaḍoro woman who heads the guild and is always travelling. You meet her on the road, never in a hall.
- **Traits:**
  - **puppeteer:** quietly steers events with what she knows;
  - **playful:** treats the world as a fascinating game.
- **Goals:**
  - keep the balance: no single power grows too strong;
  - find the court mage's papers: learn what the old empire's wizard did.
- *(Claude)* Her travelling means she can turn up anywhere in the story, and her hunt for the papers puts her on the same trail as the Wardens and the Throne's archives.

### The Lantern Company: the quartermaster
- **Who:** a Horaro quartermaster. Not the best fighter in the company, but the one who holds it together.
- **Traits:**
  - **lives by the code:** would die before breaking a contract or leaving a comrade;
  - **neutral to a fault:** works for anyone, judges no one.
- **Goals:**
  - keep the company alive: contracts, pay, and no more funerals;
  - stay neutral: avoid being dragged into the coming wars.
- *(Claude)* The code and neutrality will collide: one day a contract will put the company on the wrong side of something it can't stay out of.

### The Old Hands: the genteel old don
- **Who:** a Roduro gentleman who has never raised his voice, or a blade.
- **Traits:**
  - **politely menacing:** threats delivered as favours;
  - **loyal to the old houses:** still sees himself as their servant.
- **Goals:**
  - crush the Night Market and drive the upstart syndicate out;
  - be needed again: make the old families depend on the Old Hands.
- *(Claude)* His loyalty is mostly unreturned: the blind archivist-lord knows exactly what he's done for the houses, and won't acknowledge him.

### The Night Market: the charming fence
- **Who:** a Ṭaḍoro who runs a respectable shop by day.
- **Traits:**
  - **cheeky:** never serious, never caught;
  - **generous:** pays well and shares the take.
- **Goals:**
  - the biggest score ever: rob something legendary;
  - stay free: no faction owns the Night Market.
- *(Claude)* "Something legendary" is a ready-made heist questline: the Throne's treasury, the Holy House's relics, or the Sharp Hands' ledgers.

### The Dueling Society: the founding siblings
- **Who:** a brother and sister, Roduro and adopted Qotiro, who started the society as a lark.
- **Traits:**
  - **sporting:** respects every opponent;
  - **secretly deadly:** at least one of them has killed before, and chose to stop;
  - **crowd-pleasers:** love the spectacle;
  - **stubborn:** won't let the old heads shame them out of existence.
- **Goals:**
  - a grand tournament: all factions, one ring, one champion;
  - replace duels to the death: convince the Qotiro that sport can settle honour.
- *(Claude)* The suggested split: the Roduro brother is the showman; the adopted Qotiro sister is the one who killed, in a real duel, and started the society so no one else would have to.

## Notes
- **Every leader here is a quest-giver.** Several pairs are natural foils:
  - the freed bondswoman and the ex-slave Binder;
  - the Tide Houses' speaker and the Far Sails' speaker;
  - the two Banner Lords racing for the succession;
  - the high priest and the sincere priestess;
  - the Old Hands' don and the Night Market's fence.
- **Names:** see the table at the top.
